### C8 · India growth test — complete proposed protocol

**Question:** Can an actual message-first Hinglish demonstration help Indian students and early-career workers experience the value of Flow more effectively than a speed-focused pitch?

**Audience:** Define a small, reachable group of students/early-career users who naturally write Hindi-English messages. Avoid assuming that every Indian student uses Hinglish.

**Variant A, speed:** “Say the message. Skip typing it.”

**Variant B, expression:** “Don't stop the thought halfway through. Say it naturally.”

**Design:** Same real task and demo duration, rotate message order, prespecify what counts as success, collect consent, avoid sensitive content. Small exploratory sample with counts per variant; do not claim causal certainty. Record what objection people raise and what they actually do.

**First useful action:** Recipient creates a usable first dictation into a real app without requiring a full rewrite. **Return action:** A second useful dictation within 24 hours, confirmed appropriately, not merely app open. **Failure condition:** No improvement on first success or second use, or too-small/noisy results. **Next iteration:** Revise framing, user segment or first-message context based on the objection patterns, and test again.

**Status:** PROPOSED. No activation result, conversion lift, sample count or cohort metric claimed.

**Self-only experiment separate from user test:** Five paired tasks typed versus Flow, alternating order and recording time, edit count and whether usable. This yields a personal workflow observation, not a claim about user behavior. It has not been run in the supplied evidence.
