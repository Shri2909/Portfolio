## Step 2: PACK 1 art direction (tag `elev-after-pack-1`)
New: `src/elev/{materials,art}.js`, `src/styles/elev/{acts,materials,objects,type,chrome,night}.css`, `src/components/Split.jsx`, `src/fonts/` (Fraunces roman and italic, OFL.txt), `public/elev-boot.js`, `scripts/qa-rubric.mjs`. All CSS is scoped under `html[data-elev="1"]`; all JS is reached through `src/elev/index.js`; every added DOM node carries `data-e` and is removed by `stop()`. V3 components are untouched: lamps, windows, toran, numerals and the grade layers are augmented at runtime (a child-list MutationObserver, rAF-debounced, covers late mounts).
- **E1.1 done (with a shed):** per-act palette for sky, horizon, haze, key and fill, `--tone-w` weighting so acts 4 and 5 are not washed out, reading scrim (.82) on panels, take cards and person panels, vignette per act. Palette values live in plain variables on `html` (one style recalc per act) and each layer copies them into its own registered, non-inheriting `@property` colours, so only that layer repaints during the 1.6 s grade. Deviation: `#g-lift` and `#g-warm` (full-screen `lighten` and `soft-light` blend layers) are NOT shipped; they cost about a third of the frame rate under the software rasteriser. The lift is baked into the sky gradient instead; `#g-bloom` is a plain-alpha radial (no blend) on high only.
- **E1.2 done (with sheds):** four seamless tiles painted at idle (data URLs, no blob, nothing stored), `html.mat-ready`, `.paper`/`.grit`/`.cloth` styling applied to the existing paper surfaces (verdict note, lamp card, folders, dialogs), grain and dust layers inside `#stage`. Deviations: no `#deckle` filter (it would distort text or need a separate backing layer), grain has no blend mode and no 3-step jitter, dust has no blend mode (cut order item 2, forced by frame time); the raking-light bump is a single low-opacity fibre layer on paper (high tier) driven by `--ldx/--ldy` once E4 writes them.
- **E1.3 done (with sheds):** diya: fourth clay stop, throwing lines, fingerprint press on lamp 1, 7 glaze flecks, subsurface glow (lights up with `.is-lit`), rim light, cotton wick with twist, wet end, loose fibres and an ember dot (about 9 extra nodes per lamp, over the pack's 6). Marigold symbols (`#marigold-1..3`, `#petal-a..c`) are in the inline defs; the toran uses 14 marigolds rasterised ONCE to three tiny PNG data URLs and drawn as `<image>` (14 live vector `<use>` of about 30 petals each cost about 30 frames per scroll window because the toran swings every frame), plus leaf veins. Windows: 5 temperature colours and mullions on the near layer only; curtains, reflection streaks, interior gradient and mid-layer mullions were dropped for frame time (the skyline SVG repaints every frame for the fan and TV flicker, so each added node counts).
- **E1.4 done:** depth fixed (near darkest, far closest to the haze colour; the V3 lavender overrides are beaten by the `#skyline` id), mist by act, edge-light rims on near blocks (high only), gritty floor with 9 perspective joint lines and 5 cross joints, parapet contact shadow, plaster, 7 foreground bokeh discs (4 on mid, none on low; no filter, no blur). Deviation: `parallax.js` still writes `transform` (R5 handled in Pack 2; the camera that would compose with `--px/--py` is off).
- **E1.5 partial:** rack-focus rules (`--focus` per act, pane/halo/bokeh crossfade, one static `blur(1px)` on far) are in `camera.css` but gated by `data-camera="1"`, so they are inert until the camera passes a real-phone test. No `src/lib/camera.js` (R1).
- **E1.6 done:** Fraunces (SIL OFL 1.1) instanced and subset with fonttools: roman 36.7 KB, italic 38.3 KB, 73 KB in total (budget 90), same origin, `font-display: swap`, metric-matched `Fraunces Fallback` face, roman preloaded through the `transformIndexHtml` hook. Hinglish is the italic serif second voice (UI controls and chips keep the sans), headlines on the display serif, `324` and `19` are wrapped at runtime in `.num` and burn with the flame gradient (text-shadow removed on them: a shadow shows through a clipped fill). `Split.jsx` exists for E3 but is unused. The fallback face could not be tuned against real Georgia or Noto Serif here (the sandbox has neither); tune on a phone. `public/elev-boot.js` (a 1-line external script, allowed by the CSP) sets `data-elev` before first paint so the type and layout do not shift when elevation loads; if the elevation chunk fails to load, `main.jsx` flips it back to 0. Measured CLS on a mobile profile: 0.
